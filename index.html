Professional Profile

Engineer and data scientist who builds things people actually use — from renewable energy modelling to enterprise-scale AI, ready to bring that craft back to energy.

I’m an engineer and data scientist, and what I love most is building things people actually use. I start with the decision someone needs to make, then work backwards: shaping the solution, leading the team and owning the result.

I began in renewable energy, building SolaFuture’s first battery sizing tool, which became its standard proposal engine. At Solink, I led the battery modelling approach through the load-shedding uncertainty, then got promoted to head up systems development, taking monitoring, automation and consulting platforms through all the stages.

Now, at Sand Technologies, I lead data science and engineering on multiple projects, building the pipelines, MLOps and monitoring that keep AI systems dependable. I moved into enterprise on purpose, to learn how to build at scale, and I’m ready to bring that back to the energy sector.
